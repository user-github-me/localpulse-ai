# Chrome Web Store preparation — v1.1.0

The production package keeps permanent site access off. Optional permissions are requested
when the corresponding feature is enabled. Store text must disclose the separately connected
email service; descriptions saying "no server" or "no tracking" are obsolete.

## Suggested listing description

LocalPulse AI is a free, open-source browser workspace for understanding the pages and documents
you choose. Summarize, ask questions, rewrite selected text and study with AI that runs on your
computer whenever supported. Every answer shows whether it came from your device or the cloud.
Cloud AI is optional, uses a provider you configure, and requires your consent.

Choose from 24 actions, compare documents, check quotations against their sources, save research
collections locally, review flashcards and keep local follow-ups. Search and back up conversation
history. Read answers with installed voices. Extract text from images/scanned PDFs and transcribe
English audio locally after an explicit model-data download. Review duplicates and save tab
sessions. Optional web research sends only queries you type and fetches sources you select.

Optional email read activity is offered during onboarding. Enable it to add separate tracking
images to Gmail and Outlook drafts, turn tracking off per draft and see estimated counts and
times. Manual images are available for other apps. Tracking never sends email subjects, bodies,
recipients or private names. A separate public open-source service queues encrypted timestamps
and deletes collected events after the extension saves and acknowledges them. Private keys and
decrypted results stay in your browser, with password-encrypted backups. Image requests can be
preloads or proxy fetches and cannot prove human reading. Hosting-provider metadata policies and
free service limits apply.

There are no extension analytics or advertising. Local-only AI mode and never-send site rules
control AI requests. Separately enabled tracking, model downloads and explicit searches can
contact their disclosed services. Small models and OCR/transcription can make mistakes; review
important results. LocalPulse is free under the MIT license.

Source: https://github.com/user-github-me/localpulse-ai

## Remote code and transmitted data

All executable JavaScript and WebAssembly ships in the package. OCR, audio and language-model
downloads retrieve pinned model data, not executable code. Browser CSP permits only bundled
scripts and WebAssembly. Mozilla's static scanner also flags dependency fallback constructs;
include the source ZIP and build instructions for review instead of claiming no such constructs
exist in dependencies.

Disclose **Website content** for user-consented cloud AI requests and **Authentication information**
for API keys sent only to their configured provider. Disclose **User activity** for the optional
tracking service's image-request timestamps and explicit search queries. Tracking ciphertext
does not justify omitting that disclosure. Infrastructure necessarily handles network metadata;
the application does not record recipient IPs, location or identity. No user data is sold,
transferred for unrelated purposes or used for credit/lending decisions. Confirm the store's
current category definitions and certifications when submitting.

## Reviewer instructions (under 500 characters)

No account needed for local tools. Open LocalPulse on an article. Use Research library, Tab
organizer, Image and PDF text or Audio transcript. Model data downloads only after Enable.
Optional tracking: enable in onboarding, compose in Gmail/Outlook, inspect the per-draft toggle;
Email tracking offers a synthetic service test. Image loads estimate reads. Cloud AI requires
your own configured provider and explicit consent. Load production ZIP, not e2e build.
